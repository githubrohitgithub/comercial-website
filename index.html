About Us

Our Hospital

25 years of medical experience and expertise of the founder Dr. S.B. Gangadhar at Gurushree hospital has resulted in a much needed multi specialty state of the art, all in one medical center with highly qualified specialist doctors, trained paramedical staff and A-1 grade in-patient and out-patient facilities.

Diagnostics & assessments are the root of preventive health check up programmes that Gurushree banks upon. Exceptional clinical success rates, centuries old traditional care & warmth, but with superior technology induces one to believe that the world is an extended family — the philosophy that Gurushree believes.

Gurushree hi-tech multi speciality hospital is the only multi speciality hospital around Vijaynagar, Magadi road and Nagarbhavi within a radius of 4.5 km having facilities under one roof.

Dr. Rohith S.G. is the CEO of Gurushree Hospital.

True to the spirit of Hospitality — 'Personalised Care' experienced on hospitalisation by the patients gives the hospital an extra edge.

— Gurushree Philosophy

Importance of 'Health' is often realised during acute sickness. Saving time is saving a life. Gurushree Hi-Tech Multi-Speciality Hospital is nestled in the picturesque range of the silicon valley of India, neighbouring the Gnanabharathi University Campus popularly known as 'Nagarbhavi'.

Good Health is a priceless asset! Gurushree offers a complete range of preventive and health maintenance programs, specially designed to provide you wellness and good health.
