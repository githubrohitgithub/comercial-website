About Us

Our Hospital

Multi-specialty state of the art medical center in Nagarbhavi — the only hospital within 4.5 km with all facilities under one roof.

25 years of medical experience and expertise of the founder Dr. S.B. Gangadhar at Gurushree hospital has resulted in a much needed multi specialty state of the art, all in one medical center with highly qualified specialist doctors, trained paramedical staff and A-1 grade in-patient and out-patient facilities.

Diagnostics & assessments are the root of preventive health check up programmes that Gurushree banks upon. Exceptional clinical success rates, centuries old traditional care & warmth, but with superior technology — the philosophy that Gurushree believes.
